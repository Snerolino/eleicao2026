## 2026-08-02 - Better Keyboard Focus Indicators
**Learning:** The default browser outline can be invisible or clash with background colors. Users navigating with a keyboard benefit greatly from clear, brand-aligned `focus-visible` states that ensure visibility and consistency.
**Action:** Prioritize adding explicit `focus-visible:outline focus-visible:outline-[color]` styles on interactive elements, particularly for icon-only buttons or navigation links that rely heavily on visual cues.
